# E.I.L Product Model

## Why the product exists
E.I.L began from existential questions about the human being: who am I, what am I, why am I here, what is the purpose of human existence, what is my purpose, and what can be understood before life ends.

The system gathers years of documents, articles, books, videos, images, notes and evidence, but **the library is not the product**. The product is the transformation of accumulated knowledge into a clearer model of self, awareness and practical action.

## Primary learning loop
1. **Question** — begin with an unresolved question.
2. **Learn** — consume source-grounded material.
3. **Connect** — notice relations between previously separate ideas.
4. **Understand** — update the learner's model.
5. **Awareness** — decide what deserves foreground attention.
6. **Experiment** — test a small behavioral or perceptual change in life.
7. **Reflect** — bring real-world feedback back into the system.
8. **Revisit** — ask the original question again from a deeper layer.

## Progressive disclosure
Advanced tools should not appear because the UI has room for them. They appear when the learner has enough context.

Current thresholds are configured centrally in `src/app/navigation.ts` and should be treated as policy, not duplicated component logic.

- early journey: learning and source navigation
- after initial context: connections
- after more context: insights
- later: reflection
- later: experiments/actions
- mature journey: mentor / full synthesis

## Source hierarchy
**Level 0 — Source:** immutable full material.
**Level 1 — Structure:** headings, sections, metadata, topic labels.
**Level 2 — Connections:** relationships between sources.
**Level 3 — Insights:** derived claims with evidence.
**Level 4 — Awareness:** personal foreground focus.
**Level 5 — Experiment:** practical test in life.
**Level 6 — Reflection:** observed result.

A higher level may reference lower levels; it must never silently replace them.

## User modes
- **Creator/Owner:** full access for building, testing and curating the system.
- **Journey user:** guided progression with progressive disclosure.

## Design principle
Liquid Glass is the visual language, but it must support the journey. The design should communicate depth, layering, transparency and relationship — the same conceptual qualities as the knowledge system itself.
