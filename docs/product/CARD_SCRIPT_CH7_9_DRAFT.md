## Decision

Source documents 7–9 mix measurable biology and physics with hypotheses, spiritual traditions, metaphors and health claims. The original documents remain unchanged and readable in full. The learner layer uses a dependency sequence that makes each epistemic boundary explicit:

1. `אור, שינה ובלוטת האצטרובל` — established circadian biology first, uncertain and spiritual claims second.
2. `צליל — מפיזיקה לחוויה` — physical vibration and hearing first, experiential effects second.
3. `מפות אנרגטיות — מסורת, מטפורה ומדידה` — spiritual maps only after the learner can distinguish measurement from interpretation.

This sequence creates the bridge `EEG states → circadian mechanism → sound physics and perception → spiritual/experiential maps → neuroplastic change`.

## Claims not promoted into learning facts

- Pineal DMT release at birth, dreams, death or meditation as an established human mechanism.
- Avoiding tap water, prescribed medication or named foods as a verified way to “decalcify” the pineal gland.
- Fixed health, emotional or DNA effects for Solfeggio frequencies.
- Cymatics as proof that arbitrary sound heals the body.
- “Like attracts like” as a physical consequence of acoustic resonance.
- Chakras as anatomical centers with fixed measurable frequencies.
- Aura/Kirlian photography as a medical or emotional diagnostic.
- Schumann resonance as proof that the brain is tuned to Earth or that grounding synchronizes it.

These claims remain visible in the preserved source documents. The learning layer labels or omits them so that the platform can respect the creator’s corpus without misleading the learner.
